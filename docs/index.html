Get started

Setup takes about two minutes.

  1. InstallRun the Windows installer, or open the disk image and drag Mr. B AI into Applications.
  2. Sign inUse the default login, mrb and change-me. You can change it later in your config.json.
  3. Add your Gemini keyPaste a free key from Google AI Studio when asked. You can change it any time with the “API key” button.
  4. Ask for somethingTry “Plan a 50-minute lesson” or “Write an assignment with a rubric”. Your chats are saved in the sidebar.

If your computer shows a warning

Mr. B AI isn’t code-signed yet, so Windows and macOS ask you to confirm before opening it. This is expected.

Windows

If you see “Windows protected your PC”, choose More info, then Run anyway.

Mac

Control-click (or right-click) the app and choose Open. If it’s still blocked, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway.

Where your data goes

Chats and settings are stored on your computer only. To write replies, your messages are sent to Google’s Gemini service using your key.
